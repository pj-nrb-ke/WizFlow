# App Store / Play Store listing copy

## App name
WizFlow

## Subtitle (iOS) / Short description (Play)
Approvals and requests for your office workflow.

## Description
WizFlow Mobile lets your team approve requests, submit expenses, and track workflow status from anywhere.

**For approvers**
- Clear inbox with overdue indicators
- Approve, reject, or return with comments
- Push alerts when something needs you
- Optional approve/reject from notification actions

**For staff**
- Submit requests with dynamic forms
- Scan receipts and invoices with OCR assist
- Work offline — drafts sync when you're back online

**For managers**
- KPI snapshot and AI narrative summary on the home screen
- Anomaly alerts at a glance

Sign in with your company account. Configuration and advanced analytics remain on the web app at app.wizflow.biz.
