# WizFlow Mobile — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

WizFlow ("we", "our") provides a mobile application for workflow approvals and request submission on behalf of your employer ("your organization").

## Data we process

- **Account data:** email, name, company affiliation, and role assignments provided by your organization.
- **Workflow data:** requests, approvals, comments, attachments, and audit events you create or act on in the app.
- **Device data:** Expo push token and platform (iOS/Android) for notification delivery when you enable push alerts.
- **Optional biometrics:** Face ID / fingerprint is used only on your device to unlock the app; biometric data is not sent to our servers.

## How we use data

- Authenticate you and enforce role-based access.
- Deliver in-app and push notifications according to your preferences.
- Sync offline drafts and queued uploads when connectivity returns.
- Provide OCR-assisted field suggestions from photos you choose to capture.

## Sharing

Data is shared only with your organization's WizFlow tenant, integrated systems configured by your admin, and infrastructure providers required to operate the service (hosting, email, push relay).

## Retention

Retention follows your organization's WizFlow settings and applicable law.

## Your choices

You may disable email, in-app, or push notifications in **Settings**. You may revoke camera/microphone permissions in device settings.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact your organization's WizFlow administrator or support at the address provided on https://app.wizflow.biz.
