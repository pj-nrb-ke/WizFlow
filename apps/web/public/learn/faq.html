WizFlow FAQ — common problems, solved

Quick answers to the things people ask most. For step-by-step instructions see the How-Tos; for the full picture, the User Guide.

Signing in & your account

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen choose Forgot your password? and follow the email link. If it doesn't arrive, ask your administrator to reset it.

It says my session expired. Just sign in again — changing your password or signing out everywhere ends all active sessions, for safety.

How do I turn on two-factor authentication? Settings → Two-factor authentication → Set up, then scan the key with your authenticator app.

Requests

I submitted a request — where did it go? Open My requests. It has its own reference number and a live status; open it to see the approval chain and full timeline.

I can't see a certain request type under New request. Each process has a set list of who's allowed to start it. If you need access, ask your administrator to add you as an initiator.

My request came back as "Returned". Open it (the Returned tab), read the approver's reason, make the change, and resubmit — no need to start over.

Can I attach a file, photo or voice note? Yes — the form lets you attach documents and photos, and record a voice note that WizFlow transcribes for you.

Approvals

A request is in my inbox but I can't approve it. It may need to be claimed first (so two people don't act on it at once), or it isn't your step yet.

What's the difference between Reject and Return? Return sends it back to the requester for a change and keeps it moving; Reject ends it.

What is the AI copilot — does it decide for me? No. It summarises the request, flags anything missing or unusual, and suggests a decision. You always make the final call.

Building (managers & admins)

I built a workflow but no one can use it. Publish it — drafts aren't live — and make sure the right people are set as initiators.

My dropdown is empty. Point the field at a master-data list in the Form designer, then add entries under Master data.

My Suite isn't showing in Apps. Publish the Suite and its member processes — only published processes appear to end users.

Mobile

Do I need to download the videos? No — the video chapters stream from the server, so they don't take up space in the app.
