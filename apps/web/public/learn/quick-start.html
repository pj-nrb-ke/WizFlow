WizFlow Quick Start

Get going in about five minutes. WizFlow turns your everyday approvals — expenses, purchases, leave and more — into clean, trackable workflows. No spreadsheets, no chasing.

🎬 Prefer to watch? See Chapter 1 — Meet WizFlow in Learning Resources.

1. Sign in

Open your WizFlow address (for example app.wizflow.biz) and sign in with the email and password your administrator gave you. You'll land on Home, your daily dashboard.

2. Submit your first request

  1. In the sidebar, open Apps.
  2. Pick an application (like Expense Management) and choose the process you need — say, Purchase Request.
  3. Fill in the guided form. It only asks for what matters, and it already knows who has to approve it.
  4. Optionally attach a receipt or add a note (you can even record a voice note — WizFlow transcribes it).
  5. Press Submit request.

That's it — your request is on its way, routed to the right approvers automatically.

3. Track your request

Open My requests in the sidebar. Every request has a reference number and a clear status. Click one to see the full approval chain, who it's waiting on, and a complete timeline of everything that has happened.

4. Approve a request (when it's your turn)

If something needs your sign-off, it appears in your Inbox. 1. Open Inbox and select a request to see all its details on the right. 2. Not sure? Use the AI copilot for a quick summary and a recommended decision — you always make the final call. 3. Choose Approve, or send it back with a reason. It moves to the next step automatically.
